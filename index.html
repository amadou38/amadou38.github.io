About

PhD researcher in Lasers, Matter and Nanosciences at CELIA (Bordeaux), working on the modelling of nonlinear propagation of ultrashort intense pulses in gas-filled hollow-core fibers. I develop HPC-optimized simulation codes (UPPE and Maxwell–Schrödinger) for CPU and GPU, with a background in applied mathematics and high-performance computing.
