Modelling of Nonlinear Propagation in Hollow-Core Fibers and Applications

I develop high-performance computing codes in C++ (with the Kokkos APIs) and Python to model ultrashort pulse propagation, from an ad hoc Maxwell-based UPPE model to an ab initio approach coupled with the 1D time-dependent Schrödinger equation (1D-TDSE).

About Me

Ph.D. researcher in Lasers, Matter and Nanosciences at CELIA (Bordeaux), working on the modelling of nonlinear propagation of ultrashort intense pulses in gas-filled hollow-core fibers. I develop HPC-optimized simulation codes (UPPE and Maxwell–Schrödinger) for CPU and GPU, with a background in applied mathematics and high-performance computing.

Research Experience

Ph.D. Researcher — CELIA, University of Bordeaux (France) Mar 2023 — Present

  • Nonlinear pulse propagation code: Developed a UPPE-based simulation code in Python with fiber-mode decomposition and the split-step Fourier method, including Kerr, Raman and ionization effects.
  • Hybrid HPC code: Designed a parallel C++ code with Kokkos (CPU/GPU portability) coupling Maxwell and Schrödinger equations.
  • Supercomputers: Hands-on experience with Jean Zay (IDRIS), Irene (TGCC), Adastra (CINES), Curta (MCIA), Caliv3 (MCIA) and Theremin (CELIA).

Research Intern — Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (USA) Mar 2022 — Sep 2022

  • PML boundaries in WarpX: Research, development and implementation of the PML absorbing boundary condition in the electromagnetic particle-in-cell code WarpX (Exascale Computing Project), ATAP division.
  • Supercomputers: Perlmutter and Cori (NERSC).

M2 Modelling & Simulation with HPC — ENS Paris-Saclay Sep 2021 — Sep 2022

  • Parallel computing: Modelling and simulation with CUDA, OpenMP and MPI; performance evaluation (scalability, speed-up, efficiency).

Research Intern — CMAP, École Polytechnique Apr 2020 — Sep 2020

  • Virtual Element Method: Development and cluster implementation of the VEM method, and extension to the Hybrid High-Order (HHO) method.

Master Modelling & Scientific Computing — Cadi Ayyad University, Marrakesh 2017 — 2019

  • Master thesis: Flame propagation in an adiabatic engine system.
  • Numerical methods: GMRES, direct and iterative solvers, finite elements, finite volumes, multiscale methods.

Licence Applied Mathematics & Computer Science — Cadi Ayyad University 2014 — 2017

  • Research internship: Around Brownian motion, with Prof. Vidyadhar Mandrekar (Michigan State University).
